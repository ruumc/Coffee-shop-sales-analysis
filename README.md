# Coffee-shop-sales-analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes transactional sales data from a coffee shop in order to understand customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends over time. The main objective is to extract actionable insights that can support business decision-making in areas such as product strategy, demand planning, and operational optimization.

## Objectives
Clean and preprocess raw sales data
Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
Identify top-performing products and revenue drivers
Analyze sales trends over time (monthly and weekly patterns)
Compare product categories (food vs beverages)
Explore relationships between variables using correlation analysis
Extract actionable business insights

## Dataset
The dataset contains transactional sales records including information such as: Date and time of purchase, Product name and category, Quantity sold, and Price and revenue data.

## Tools & Technologies
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib / Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## Methodology
Data cleaning and handling missing values  
Feature engineering (time-based variables such as month and weekday)  
Exploratory data analysis (EDA)  
Revenue and product performance analysis  
Temporal analysis (monthly and weekly trends)  
Correlation analysis between numerical variables  
Visualization of key patterns  

## Key Insights
Top-performing products: Salad is the most profitable item, indicating high revenue efficiency in this category.   
Most purchased product: Juice is the most frequently purchased item, showing strong customer preference in volume, although not necessarily in revenue contribution.    
Product category performance: Food products generate higher total revenue compared to beverages, indicating that food items are the main revenue driver of the business, despite differences in purchase frequency between categories.

Seasonality and time trends: Sales show clear temporal variation. February is the lowest-performing month, while June and October are the strongest months, indicating seasonal effects in customer demand.  
Weekly patterns: Sales are not evenly distributed throughout the week. Thursday and Friday consistently show the highest sales volume, suggesting increased demand towards the end of the week.

Correlation analysis: A correlation analysis between numerical variables was conducted; however, no strong linear relationships were identified, suggesting that sales drivers may be non-linear or influenced by external factors not captured in the dataset.

## Conclusion
This analysis provides a clear understanding of sales behavior in a coffee shop environment. The insights can support decision-making in areas such as inventory management, marketing strategy, staffing, and product optimization.
